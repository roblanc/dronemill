NofaceChan uploader

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel @NofaceChan55.

This application is used only by the owner of the NofaceChan channel. It uploads the channel's own videos and Shorts, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publication times, and reads the channel's list of uploads so that new videos are scheduled into free days.

It is not offered to the public and does not collect information about anyone else.
