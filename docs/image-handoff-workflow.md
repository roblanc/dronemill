# Image Handoff Workflow

The minimal handoff is: `I uploaded a new image. Check it and use it for a
video.` The filename is helpful but not required.

## Intake

1. Locate recent image or video assets in CasaOS storage.
2. Open the asset visually and identify its setting, subjects, palette,
   perspective, atmosphere, implied sounds, and likely motion boundaries.
3. Verify resolution, aspect ratio, codec, and file integrity.
4. Preserve the original upload and work from a production copy.

## Creative Proposal

Choose the following from the image rather than from a fixed template:

- Emotional premise and title family.
- Clean, film-soft, VHS-memory, or VHS-decay packaging.
- Camera treatment, scene-aware overlays, or Midjourney animation request.
- Harmonic language, environmental layers, duration, and mastering target.
- Thumbnail frame and public-facing description.

## Default Audio Rule

Do not add beeps, blips, pings, chimes, isolated droplets, notification-like
tones, or random one-shot sounds. Prefer continuous evolving harmony and
environmental texture whose source is clearly implied by the scene. Exceptions
require explicit user approval.

Use `docs/title-and-visual-packaging.md` for title and overlay selection.

## Review Gate

Before long-form production, publish a 60- to 120-second audiovisual sample in
`/srv/media/videos/DroneMill Previews/`. The user can approve it or request
specific changes to motion, music, effects, loudness, or packaging.

Do not start an expensive long-form render or public upload until the concept
sample is approved, unless the user explicitly requests full autonomy.

## Production

After approval:

1. Render unique continuous long-form audio; do not repeat the review sample.
2. Build scene-appropriate motion without stacking unnecessary effects.
3. Validate duration, frame rate, codecs, loudness, true peak, and loop seams.
4. Generate a clean thumbnail and editorial description.
5. Upload privately or scheduled, verify monetization and ad placement, then
   publish according to the requested schedule.
6. Update local upload history, scheduler state, creative recipes, and GitHub.
7. Delete temporary production files only after upload success is confirmed;
   retain the approved media-library copy.
