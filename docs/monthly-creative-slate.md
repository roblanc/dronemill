# Monthly Creative Slate

Alternate themes so adjacent releases remain visually distinct. All soundtracks
use continuous evolving music and scene-implied textures with no beeps, blips,
pings, chimes, or random one-shot sounds. Prompts assume `--ar 16:9 --style raw`.
